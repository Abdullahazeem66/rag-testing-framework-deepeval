# Billing and Refunds

## Payment methods

Orbitly accepts major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express). Customers on annual Business or Enterprise plans with a yearly total above $5,000 can request to pay by invoice, with payment due within 30 days (net 30). PayPal and cryptocurrency are not accepted.

## Refund policy

Annual subscriptions can be refunded in full if the cancellation request is made within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, annual subscriptions are not refundable, but the plan remains active until the end of the paid term. Monthly subscriptions are never refunded; cancelling a monthly plan stops the next renewal.

## Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and the price difference is prorated for the remainder of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period. If a downgrade puts a workspace above the new plan's limits (for example more than 3 active projects on Free), the extra projects become read-only.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, Orbitly retries the charge and sends email reminders during a 14-day grace period. If payment is still not received after 14 days, the workspace is switched to read-only mode until the balance is paid. Data is never deleted because of a failed payment.

## Taxes and invoices

Prices are shown excluding VAT and sales tax, which are added at checkout based on the billing address. Invoices can be downloaded from Settings > Billing > Invoice history.
