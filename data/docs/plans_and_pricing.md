# Plans and Pricing

Orbitly is a project management platform for teams. It is offered in four plans: Free, Pro, Business and Enterprise.

## Free plan

The Free plan costs nothing and is intended for small teams getting started. It supports up to 5 members per workspace, up to 3 active projects and 1 GB of total file storage. Guests cannot be invited on the Free plan. Support on the Free plan is limited to the community forum.

## Pro plan

The Pro plan costs $12 per user per month when billed annually, or $15 per user per month when billed monthly. Pro includes unlimited projects, unlimited members, 100 GB of file storage per workspace, timeline (Gantt) views, custom fields and up to 10 guests per workspace.

## Business plan

The Business plan costs $24 per user per month when billed annually, or $29 per user per month when billed monthly. Business includes everything in Pro, plus SAML single sign-on (SSO), audit logs, time tracking, unlimited guests and 1 TB of file storage per workspace.

## Enterprise plan

Enterprise pricing is custom and quoted by the sales team. Enterprise includes everything in Business, plus data residency options, a dedicated customer success manager (CSM), 24/7 phone support and a 99.95% uptime SLA. Enterprise requires a minimum of 50 seats.

## Discounts

Registered nonprofit organizations receive a 50% discount on Pro and Business plans. Accredited educational institutions can use the Pro plan for free for classroom use. Discounts cannot be combined with other promotions.
