# Support and SLA

## Support channels by plan

Free plan users are supported through the community forum only. Pro customers get email support with a first response within 24 hours, Monday to Friday. Business customers get priority email and live chat support with a first response within 4 hours, 24 hours a day, 5 days a week. Enterprise customers get 24/7 phone support, a dedicated customer success manager, and a 1-hour first response for Severity 1 incidents.

## Uptime SLA

Orbitly guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime on the Business plan and 99.95% monthly uptime on the Enterprise plan. Free and Pro plans have no uptime SLA. Scheduled maintenance announced at least 48 hours in advance does not count as downtime.

## Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below the SLA, customers receive a service credit of 10% of the monthly fee for each 0.1% below the guaranteed uptime, up to a maximum of 50% of the monthly fee. Credits must be requested within 30 days of the end of the affected month.

Current system status and incident history are published at status.orbitly.example.
