# Features Guide

## Boards and views

Every project can be viewed as a Kanban board, a list or a calendar on all plans. Timeline (Gantt) views are available on Pro and above.

## Automations

Automations run actions when a trigger happens, such as moving a task when its status changes. The number of automation runs per month depends on the plan: Free includes 100 runs, Pro includes 5,000 runs, Business includes 50,000 runs and Enterprise includes unlimited runs. Unused runs do not roll over to the next month.

## Custom fields

Custom fields (text, number, date, dropdown and person fields) are available on Pro and above. Each project can have up to 50 custom fields.

## Guests

Guests are people outside your organization who can view and comment on projects they are invited to. Guests cannot create projects. Pro workspaces can invite up to 10 guests; Business and Enterprise workspaces can invite unlimited guests. Guests are not available on the Free plan.

## Time tracking

Time tracking lets members log hours against tasks and export timesheets as CSV. Time tracking is available on the Business and Enterprise plans.
