# Troubleshooting

## Notifications not arriving

If email notifications are not arriving, check your spam folder, then check Profile > Notifications to make sure email notifications are enabled. Make sure emails from notifications@orbitly.example are allowed by your mail server.

## Desktop app not syncing

If the desktop app is not syncing, open Help > Reset local cache and restart the app. The desktop app is available for Windows 10 and later and macOS 13 and later.

## Mobile app requirements

The Orbitly mobile app requires iOS 16 or later, or Android 10 or later. Offline mode on mobile lets you view and edit tasks; changes sync when you reconnect.

## File upload limits

The maximum size for a single uploaded file is 25 MB on the Free plan and 250 MB on all paid plans. Uploads that exceed the limit fail with the error "File too large".

## Locked out of your account

If you were locked out after too many failed sign-in attempts, wait 15 minutes and try again, or reset your password using the "Forgot password" link.
