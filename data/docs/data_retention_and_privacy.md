# Data Retention and Privacy

## Deleted items

Deleted tasks, files and projects move to the Trash, where they can be restored for 30 days. After 30 days, items in the Trash are permanently deleted.

## Deleting a workspace

When a workspace owner deletes a workspace, it can be restored by contacting support within 14 days. After 14 days, the workspace and all of its data are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

## Backups

Orbitly takes encrypted backups every 24 hours and keeps them for 35 days. Backups are used for disaster recovery only and cannot be used to restore individual items on request.

## Data residency

By default, customer data is stored in the United States. Enterprise customers can choose to store data in the European Union (Frankfurt) or Australia (Sydney). Data residency cannot be changed on Free, Pro or Business plans.

## Exporting data

Workspace admins on any plan can export all workspace data at any time as JSON or CSV from Settings > Data > Export. Exports are emailed as a download link that is valid for 7 days.

## GDPR

Orbitly acts as a data processor for customer content and offers a Data Processing Agreement (DPA), which can be signed from Settings > Legal. Data subject requests can be sent to privacy@orbitly.example.
